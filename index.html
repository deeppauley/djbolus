Trillium is a DJ and electronic music producer whose sound reflects decades immersed in electronic music. Drawing from the open-minded spirit of Ibiza’s Balearic tradition, his work moves between house and techno, combining hypnotic rhythms, deep basslines, and evolving melodic textures with an emphasis on groove, atmosphere, and subtle tension.

After first discovering Ibiza in 2000, Trillium spent several seasons immersed in the island’s club culture while making regular trips to London, Berlin, and Detroit’s Movement Festival. Those experiences shaped a musical perspective informed by some of electronic music’s most influential artists, clubs, and festivals.

As a producer, Trillium channels those influences into original compositions built around intricate percussion, driving low-end, and layered synthesizers. His productions balance rhythmic intensity with melodic depth, allowing musical ideas to develop gradually through shifting textures, evolving arrangements, and carefully controlled tension and release. Moving between groove-driven house and more atmospheric, melodic techno, his music draws on the dance floor while maintaining a distinctive sonic character.

Behind the decks, Trillium brings the same sensibility to his selections, blending house music, tribal rhythms, and techno into fluid, immersive sets. Favoring patient progression over obvious peaks, he builds momentum through subtle transitions, rhythmic interplay, and an instinctive understanding of the dance floor.

Whether producing original music or performing live, Trillium’s focus remains on creating electronic music that is hypnotic, immersive, and driven by the interplay of rhythm, melody, and atmosphere.
